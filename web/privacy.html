Privacy Policy for udu's chat

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

udu's chat ("we", "our", or "the app") provides real-time 1-on-1 and group messaging over the internet at https://udu-s-chat-web.vercel.app.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you sign in with Google or Email/Password, we collect your email address and the display name and "About" status you choose.

Your information is used solely to authenticate your account, allow other users to find your chosen display name, and deliver messages in real time. We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for advertising.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

You can delete your own messages at any time inside the app, or contact us to request full account deletion.
